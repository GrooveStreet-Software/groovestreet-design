About Us

Quality Care in Mbale

GrooveStreet Clinic is a modern healthcare facility in the heart of Mbale, Uganda, dedicated to delivering exceptional medical care with compassion and integrity. Our team of specialist doctors, state-of-the-art equipment, and patient-centered approach make us the trusted choice for families across the region.

From routine check-ups to advanced specialized treatments, we provide comprehensive healthcare services under one roof. Our mission is simple: to keep every patient healthy, treated with dignity, and cared for as family.

Our Services

Comprehensive Healthcare

From prevention to treatment, we offer a full range of medical services for every stage of life.

General Practice

Primary care for all ages, including routine check-ups, consultations, and health screenings.

Pediatrics

Maternity & Midwifery
