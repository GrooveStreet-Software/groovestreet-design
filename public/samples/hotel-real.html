Our Story

A Landmark Address in the Heart of Kampala

Hotel GrooveStreet began as a rooftop bar beloved by the city's early nightlife crowd. Two decades later, it has grown into a 64-room boutique hotel that pairs the energy of live music with the calm of a private spa.

Every suite is hand-finished with local materials, every window frames the lake, and our kitchen draws on Ugandan produce delivered from family farms in the highlands.

Kamala OkothGeneral Manager
