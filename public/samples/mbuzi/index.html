Our Story

We Invite You to Visit
Our Mbuzi Choma Spot

Located in the heart of Mbarara on High Street, YPA Mbuzi Choma brings you 100% Halal, fresh goat meat roasted to smoky perfection over open charcoal. Paired with crisp kachumbari and hearty Ugandan staples, every plate is a true taste of home.

Fresh Local Goat Daily

We source top-grade Ankole and local goats every morning, ensuring every cut is naturally tender, clean, and 100% Halal.

Master Open-Fire Roasting

Our grill masters slow-roast over natural charcoal, locking in natural juices while building a rich, smoky crust.

Dine-In, Delivery & Events

Whether you are dining on High Street, ordering lunch to your office, or booking catering for a function, we serve fast.
