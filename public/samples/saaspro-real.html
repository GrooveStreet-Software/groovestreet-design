Is there a free trial?

Yes — every plan includes a full 14-day trial of the Growth plan with no credit card required. Nothing is charged until you choose to upgrade.

Can I migrate my existing data?

One-click importers are available for the most common tools, and our team will run a guided migration for you at no cost on annual plans.

How does pricing scale?

Growth is billed per active user, per month. You can add or remove seats at any time and are only charged for the seats you use.

Is my data secure?

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. SAASPRO is SOC 2 Type II certified and offers SSO, SCIM provisioning and granular audit logs on every plan.

Do you offer support for developers?

Every plan includes API access, webhooks and a typed SDK. Developer documentation and a sandbox environment are available from day one.

Can I cancel at any time?

Absolutely. Cancel from your billing settings and you will keep full access until the end of the billing period — no lock-in, no cancellation fees.
